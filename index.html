Scans Polymarket's mutually-exclusive ("negRisk") event books in your browser. If buying every outcome costs under $1.00 total, one of them must pay $1.00 — that gap is arbitrage. No signup.

YES-set cost = buy one share of every outcome (sum of asks) → pays exactly $1 when any leg resolves. NO edge = profit per $1 stake from buying every NO instead. Negative means the book is already overpriced (arb is the other direction). Filter: events with ≥$5k volume in 24h, sorted closest to free money.
